# Compliance & monetization notes

> Reference only — not legal, tax or financial advice. Policies change. Each fact below carries a status: **search_snippet** = seen in snippets of the official YouTube/AdSense help page via web search on 2026-10-10 (the pages themselves could not be opened from the build environment); **conflicting** = sources disagreed; **unverified** = recalled / commonly reported. The same facts are editable data in `server/config/policy.js` and shown (with status badges) on the *Monetization* page.

## Three different things
1. **Monetization eligibility (YouTube Partner Program)** — channel level.
2. **Made for kids** — a per-video legal designation (COPPA etc.) you must set truthfully.
3. **YouTube Kids app inclusion** — a separate curated app; no application; being made-for-kids is only a prerequisite.

## YouTube Partner Program (search_snippet)
- Full programme: **1,000 subscribers** + **4,000 public watch hours (12 months)** *or* **10 M public Shorts views (90 days)**.
- Early-access tier (fan funding / shopping, not ads): 500 subscribers + 3 public uploads (90 days) + 3,000 hours or 3 M Shorts views.
- Also: follow channel monetization policies, no active Community Guidelines strikes, 2-Step Verification, linked AdSense, manual review (meeting thresholds ≠ approval).
- **Announced change:** from **1 Feb 2027** new applicants need **8,000 watch hours / 365 days** or **20 M Shorts views / 90 days** (reported by a YouTube Blog result — verify).

## Originality (search_snippet)
- Since 15 July 2025 "repetitious content" became **inauthentic content**: mass-produced/template-based/repetitive videos — including AI-generated templated videos with no original insight — can lose monetization for the **whole channel**. The compliance gate therefore checks script similarity between your videos, warns when many videos share one kit, and requires you to confirm *original creative input*.
- Altered/synthetic-content disclosure is required for **realistic** altered/synthetic media; unrealistic animation and production help (scripting, captions) don't need it. EA KIDS videos are stylised cartoons — you decide; disclose realistic AI footage/voices of real people.

## Made for kids (search_snippet)
- Turns off comments, personalised ads, notifications, live chat/Super Chat, memberships; **contextual ads still run**; revenue may be lower.
- You set it; YouTube may override. Never choose "not for kids" to avoid restrictions. The studio flags child-directed content that is marked otherwise.

## Getting paid from Iraq (search_snippet — **verify in your AdSense account**)
- Payment methods listed for Iraq: **check ✔, wire transfer ✔, EFT ✘, Hyperwallet ✘** (the table may be outdated).
- Payment threshold ≈ **$100** (reporting-currency based; the dinar figure wasn't found). $10 needed before you can select a method.
- Payout currency is USD or EUR set by Google (Iraq's wasn't confirmed). Bank account must be in your own country; only your bank can confirm it receives international wires; allow up to ~15 business days; your bank/intermediaries may charge fees and apply their own FX rates → ask for **SWIFT/BIC**, incoming-wire fee and conversion rate.
- AdSense identity/address (PIN) verification and **tax information** may be required (unverified details).
- **Never** use another person's identity, falsify residency, or try to bypass regional restrictions — it can permanently close the accounts.

## What the studio enforces before an upload
Scenes approved · facts verified by a human · review checklist complete · no personal-information prompts · licences recorded for imported media · final video fresh + QC passed · thumbnail ≤ 2 MB 16:9 · metadata honest (no clickbait/stuffing) · audience designated and justified · owner confirmations · script not a near-duplicate of another · compliance approval still valid · dry-run reviewed · owner types `UPLOAD`.
