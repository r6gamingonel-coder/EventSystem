# Characters in this episode

## ريّان — Rayyan (Lion cub)

A brave, curious lion cub with a golden mane and a teal scarf. Rayyan is the friendly guide who introduces every lesson.

- **Look:** Round golden head with a scalloped orange mane, small round ears with pink inside, cream muzzle, brown heart-shaped nose, teal scarf tied at the neck, tail with an orange tuft.
- **Colours:** body #FFB84D, mane #E07B2A, scarf #3FA9F5, muzzle #FFF6E0
- **Personality:** Brave, curious, encouraging. Always asks the audience a question and celebrates answers.
- **Educational role:** Host / guide — introduces the topic and recaps what was learned.
- **Voice:** warm and upbeat. Use a licensed TTS voice or a voice actor you have a written agreement with. Do not clone a real person.

## نونو — Nunu (Bunny)

A gentle lavender bunny with a pink bow. Nunu loves counting and always shares.

- **Look:** Lavender body, very tall ears with pink inner, round face, pink bow on the right ear, two small front teeth, white pom-pom tail.
- **Colours:** body #EADFFF, inner #FFB3C7, bow #FF5C8A
- **Personality:** Gentle, kind, a little shy but loves to share and help.
- **Educational role:** Counting, numbers and kindness lessons.
- **Voice:** soft and kind. Licensed TTS voice or consenting voice actor only.

## زقزق — Zaqzaq (Chick)

A tiny round yellow chick who loves songs, sounds and surprises.

- **Look:** Perfectly round yellow body, three-feather head tuft, small orange beak, tiny wings, thin orange legs with three toes.
- **Colours:** body #FFD23F, beak #FF8A1F, belly #FFF0A8
- **Personality:** Cheerful, energetic, giggles a lot. Loves animal sounds and songs.
- **Educational role:** Songs, animal sounds and alphabet sounds.
- **Voice:** bubbly. Licensed TTS voice or consenting voice actor only.

## سلّومة — Sallouma (Turtle)

A calm, wise little turtle with round glasses and a green hexagon-patterned shell.

- **Look:** Dome-shaped green shell with darker hexagon pattern, light-green round head, small round glasses, stubby feet.
- **Colours:** body #A8E063, shell #3CB371, belly #F3D98B
- **Personality:** Calm, patient, loves nature facts. Speaks slowly and clearly.
- **Educational role:** Nature, science and good-habits lessons.
- **Voice:** slow and calm. Licensed TTS voice or consenting voice actor only.
